=====================================================
UNDER 40 REPORT DASHBOARD - v1.3 PORTABLE PACKAGE
=====================================================

HOW TO RUN ON WINDOWS:
1. Double-click "Under40Report.exe".
2. The application opens automatically in a clean desktop window.
3. No installation, admin rights, or extra software required.

HOW TO RUN ON MAC / LINUX:
- Double-click "start_mac.command" OR open "index.html" in Google Chrome, Safari, or Edge.

KEY FEATURES & WORKFLOW:

1. WEEKLY PROCESSING:
   - Drag & drop your two weekly CSV reports onto the screen:
     * Report 1: Working Hours CSV
     * Report 2: Leaves & Sick Leaves CSV
   - The dashboard matches candidates, computes missing hours, and lists those under 40 hours.
   - Enter Client Name, Dispatch Fault, Form of Pay, Informed Client, and Comments.

2. REMOVING PART-TIMERS:
   - Check the checkbox next to any part-timer and click "Delete Part-Timer(s)".
   - You can restore deleted candidates at any time using "Part-Timers (X)".

3. GRAND SUMMARY (MONTHLY CONSOLIDATION):
   - Switch to the "Grand Summary" tab.
   - Upload multiple weekly saved reports (.json) to consolidate hours over a month.
   - Filter or sort candidate names (A-Z, Z-A) and total missing hours.
   - Click "Send to Summary Report" to transfer the consolidated data into the Summary Report.
   - Click "Clear Weeks" to reset the monthly data.

4. DEDICATED SUMMARY REPORT:
   - Summary Report operates completely independently from the weekly raw data.
   - Export CSV: Clean CSV download of summary records.
   - Export PDF: Clean white paper audit document with candidate names and figures (no graphics).
   - Clear Summary: Clears the summary report when you are ready for the next batch.

5. CLEAN WHITE PAPER PDF REPORTS:
   - Every PDF export now features a clean, high-contrast white paper report.
   - All reports open an interactive print preview first, so you can review before saving or printing.

6. HOW TO GET UPDATES:
   - Click "File" > "Check for Updates..." at any time.
   - The app also checks silently in the background when connected to the internet.
   - When an update is ready, a banner appears with the new version and a 1-click download link.
   - If offline, the app continues to work normally with zero interruptions.

=====================================================
Privacy & Security:
- 100% Client-Side. Your payroll and employee data stays on your computer and is never sent across the internet.
